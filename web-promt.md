ABOUT KENFLO
Emotional Wellness for Everyday Life
Emotional wellness affects how we respond to stress, communicate with others, navigate
relationships, make decisions, manage transitions, and participate in our families and
communities.
Yet not every difficult season of life requires clinical mental health treatment.
Sometimes people need a supportive environment where they can reflect, learn, strengthen
practical skills, identify available resources, and determine their next steps.
KENFLO Emotional Health Services LLC exists to help meet that need.We provide accessible, stigma-free and culturally responsive non-clinical emotional wellness
services focused on education, prevention, resilience, communication, personal
development, family strengthening, coping skills, and community well-being.
We also recognize the limits of non-clinical services. When someone's needs appear to require
licensed psychotherapy, clinical assessment, psychiatric care, substance-use treatment, medical
care, crisis services, or another specialized service, KENFLO encourages and facilitates
connection with appropriately qualified professionals and community resources.

CULTURAL & IMMIGRATION ADJUSTMENT
Culture Matters in Emotional Wellness
People experience life through the context of family, culture, community, language, faith
traditions, relationships, migration experiences, values, and personal history.
Moving between cultures can create opportunities—but also significant adjustment.
Families may experience changing roles, parent-child differences, isolation, communication
challenges, identity questions, unfamiliar systems, workplace pressures, and differences between
traditional expectations and life in a new environment.
KENFLO provides culturally responsive, non-clinical support and education for individuals and
families navigating these transitions.

IMPORTANT SERVICE INFORMATION
Non-Clinical Scope of Services
KENFLO Emotional Health Services LLC provides non-clinical emotional wellness support,
coaching, education, skills development, prevention programming, workshops, and
resource navigation.
KENFLO's non-clinical services are not represented as psychotherapy, professional clinical
counseling, psychological or psychiatric diagnosis, clinical assessment, medical treatment,
psychiatric treatment, or emergency crisis care.Participation in KENFLO services does not establish a psychotherapist-patient or physician-
patient relationship.
When an individual's circumstances indicate that licensed clinical, medical, emergency, or
specialized professional services may be appropriate, KENFLO may recommend or facilitate
connection with appropriately qualified providers or community resources.
Any clinical service offered in the future would be separately identified and provided only by an
appropriately licensed professional acting within that professional's authorized scope of practice.
EMERGENCY & CRISIS NOTICE
KENFLO Is Not an Emergency or Crisis-Response Service
Do not use website forms, email, voicemail, coaching sessions, or KENFLO non-clinical
services to obtain emergency assistance.
If you or another person is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency
department.
If you are experiencing a suicidal, mental-health, or substance-use crisis, call or text 988 to
reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

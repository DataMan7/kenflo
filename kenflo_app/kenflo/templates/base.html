Scope of Services

KENFLO Emotional Health Services LLC provides non-clinical emotional wellness support, coaching, education, skills development, prevention programming, workshops, and resource navigation. Services are not psychotherapy, professional clinical counseling, psychological or psychiatric diagnosis, clinical assessment, medical treatment, or emergency crisis care.

Serving Minnesota

Individuals · Couples & Families · Parents · Teens & Youth · Groups · Schools · Churches & Faith Communities · Nonprofits · Workplaces · Multicultural & Immigrant Communities.

Need immediate help? KENFLO is not an emergency or crisis-response service. If there is immediate danger, call 911. For immediate emotional or mental-health support, call or text 988 (available 24/7 in Minnesota for emotional or mental-health distress, including suicide and substance-use concerns).

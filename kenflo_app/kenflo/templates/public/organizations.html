  • Emotional wellness events
  • Community education initiatives

Community Leader Programs

Educational programs for community and faith leaders that build awareness of emotional wellness needs, strengthen everyday support skills, and connect community members with appropriate resources. These programs do not train participants to diagnose mental-health conditions or practice psychotherapy — they help community leaders become informed and responsible bridges to appropriate support.

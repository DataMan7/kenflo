Groups & Workshops

Practical Education. Connection. Reflection. Emotional Wellness Skills.

KENFLO brings people together in supportive group settings to learn and practice everyday emotional wellness skills. Group offerings can be customized for communities, workplaces, faith communities, and youth-serving organizations.

Wellness & Prevention Services

  • Stress-Management Coaching — breathing and relaxation exercises, grounding strategies, guided reflection, stress-awareness practices, healthy routines, emotional self-regulation, and practical coping strategies.
  • Mind-Body Wellness — reflective journaling, relaxation exercises, mindful awareness, mental-clarity routines, self-reflection, healthy lifestyle practices, and personal wellness planning.
  • Personal Support & Resource Planning — a practical personal support plan identifying sources of social support, healthy coping strategies, personal warning signs, trusted contacts, community and professional resources, and steps for seeking additional assistance.

Workshop Topics

  • Emotional intelligence & awareness
  • Resilience building
  • Stress management & burnout prevention
  • Trauma awareness (educational)
  • Healthy communication
  • Conflict management
  • Grief & loss education
  • Customized workshops on request
Please note: these are wellness practices and educational programs. They are not psychotherapy or clinical treatment, and they do not replace professional crisis assessment, emergency services, or clinical safety planning.

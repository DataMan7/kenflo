Cultural & Immigration Adjustment

Culture Matters in Emotional Wellness

People experience life through the context of family, culture, community, language, faith traditions, relationships, migration experiences, values, and personal history. Moving between cultures can create opportunities — but also significant adjustment.

What We Support

  • Cultural adjustment for individuals and families
  • Changing family roles across cultures
  • Identity and belonging
  • Intergenerational communication
  • Parent-youth adjustment differences
  • Community connection
  • Resource navigation

Our Approach

Practical, culturally responsive conversations and skills-building that respect your family's values and experiences. Support is provided in a stigma-free environment, and we help families identify appropriate professional and community resources when additional care is needed.

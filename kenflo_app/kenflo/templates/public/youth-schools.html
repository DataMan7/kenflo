Youth & Schools

Helping Young People Build Skills for Life

Young people are navigating a rapidly changing world. Social relationships, technology, school expectations, family changes, cultural transitions, peer pressure, identity development, and uncertainty about the future can create significant pressures.

KENFLO offers age-appropriate non-clinical coaching and emotional wellness education designed to strengthen resilience and practical life skills.

Teen Emotional Wellness Coaching

  • Confidence and personal development
  • Identity and belonging
  • Peer-pressure skills
  • Healthy technology habits
  • Stress-management skills
  • Emotional awareness and communication
  • Decision-making and goal setting
  • Resilience and life transitions

School Partnership Programs

  • Student resilience programs
  • Emotional wellness education
  • Anti-bullying education
  • Healthy communication workshops
  • Stress-management skills
  • Emotional intelligence
  • Healthy peer relationships
  • Cultural adjustment education
  • Parent education & staff wellness education
Safeguarding: services involving minors are provided according to applicable consent, safeguarding, privacy, reporting, and organizational policies. Programs may be customized in collaboration with schools and community organizations.
